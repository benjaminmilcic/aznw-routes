# Webaro (Portfolio project)

## What is Webaro?
Webaro (WEBARO Cloud, webaro.dev) is a web app of the company Weber Agrar Robotik GmbH. It is used for the efficient planning and evaluation of drone data in agriculture and shows a dashboard with real-time data.

## What was Benjamin's role in Webaro?
For Weber Agrar Robotik GmbH, on behalf of Codext GmbH, Benjamin worked on the further development of the Webaro web app for planning and evaluating drone data in agriculture. It is built with Angular. The portfolio on his website shows screenshots of the dashboard, the interface and the analytics and links to webaro.dev. Further details of his work on the project are not described. It is a client project: its source code is not public and there is no live demo.
