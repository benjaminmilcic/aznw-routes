# CatchCups (Portfolio project)

## What is CatchCups?
CatchCups is a product for checking how evenly a lawn is watered (an irrigation audit). It consists of a set of physical catch cups that are placed on the lawn and catch the water of the sprinklers, and the CatchCups app, which evaluates the caught amounts of water. According to its website (catchcups.com) it works with all above-ground irrigation systems and is meant for homeowners and lawn owners; the method is the standard audit used for golf courses and sports fields. The app is available for iOS and Android.

## What does the CatchCups app do?
According to the CatchCups website, the app offers:
- four test types: Random, Grid (with heatmap), Hotspot (3×3 layout) and Edge (compared to a reference cup)
- a dashboard with the Distribution Uniformity (DU) of each measurement
- a heatmap of the measured water distribution
- over- and under-watering in percent, quartile and extreme-value overviews
- zone management and a guide to how much extra water dry spots need
