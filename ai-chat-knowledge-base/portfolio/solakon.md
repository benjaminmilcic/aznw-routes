# Solakon (Portfolio project)

## What is Solakon?
Solakon is a German provider of plug-and-play mini solar systems (balcony power plants) and battery storage, based in Schwäbisch Hall, Germany. According to its website (solakon.de) it sells systems for private households – for balconies, garage roofs, facades and gardens – and develops the product concept, firmware and app itself. Solakon is a client of Codext GmbH.

## What does the Solakon app do?
The Solakon app is used to control and monitor a Solakon solar system: it integrates balcony power plants into the home energy network. It is available for iOS and Android and is live in the Google Play Store. The Solakon website also mentions energy management and smart-meter integration.

## What was Benjamin's role in Solakon?
On behalf of the company Codext GmbH, Benjamin worked on the Solakon mobile app, which enables the integration of balcony power plants into the home energy network. The app is built with Angular and Ionic. The portfolio on his website shows screenshots and links to solakon.de and the app in the Google Play Store. Further details of his work on the project are not described. It is a client project: its source code is not public and there is no live demo.
