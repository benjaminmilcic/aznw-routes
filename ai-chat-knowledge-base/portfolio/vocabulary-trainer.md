# Vocabulary Trainer (Portfolio project)

## What is the Vocabulary Trainer?
The Vocabulary Trainer is Benjamin's own app for learning the Croatian language, as a web app and as an Android app. It contains 300 verbs, 500 nouns and 150 adjectives in Croatian and German, organized in lessons. Each word comes with an example sentence and its translation; verbs also show their conjugation (present and past tense), nouns their gender and plural, adjectives their feminine and neuter forms.

## What can you do in the Vocabulary Trainer?
- **Learn:** go through the words of a lesson, from Croatian to German or from German to Croatian
- **Quiz:** answer questions about the words
- **Type:** type the translation yourself
- **List:** look up all words of a category with their details

## How is the Vocabulary Trainer built?
The app is built with Angular and Ionic; the Android app is packaged with Capacitor and can be downloaded as an APK. The web app is hosted on Firebase and has a live demo. The source code is public on GitHub in the repository learn-croatian. The portfolio on Benjamin's website links the live demo, the APK download and the source code.
