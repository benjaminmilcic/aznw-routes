# Hardware

## Which computers does Benjamin work with?
Benjamin works with two Lenovo laptops, both with Windows 11:
- A Lenovo IdeaPad Flex 5 (15.6" convertible) with an AMD Ryzen 7 5700U (8 cores, 16 threads), 16 GB RAM and a 512 GB NVMe SSD. At his desk it runs three screens: its own 15.6" display and two external 24" monitors, a Philips 246B1 and a Fujitsu B24-8 TE Pro, all in Full HD (1920 × 1080).
- A Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 3 with an Intel Core i5-1245U (10 cores, 12 threads), 16 GB RAM, a 256 GB NVMe SSD and a 14" display with 1920 × 1200.
He types on a Logitech MX Keys Mini, a compact wireless Bluetooth keyboard.

## What does the Lenovo IdeaPad Flex 5 setup look like in detail?
The Lenovo IdeaPad Flex 5 15ALC05 has an AMD Ryzen 7 5700U processor with integrated AMD Radeon graphics, 16 GB DDR4-3200 RAM (2 × 8 GB), a Micron 2450 NVMe SSD with 512 GB and runs Windows 11 Home. The two external 24" monitors (Philips 246B1 and Fujitsu B24-8 TE Pro, each 1920 × 1080 at 60 Hz) are connected through a DisplayLink docking station.

## What does the Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 3 look like in detail?
The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 3 has a 12th gen Intel Core i5-1245U processor with Intel UHD graphics, 16 GB DDR4-3200 RAM, an SK hynix NVMe SSD with 256 GB and a 14" display with 1920 × 1200, and runs Windows 11 Pro.

## Which keyboard does Benjamin use?
Benjamin uses a Logitech MX Keys Mini: a compact wireless keyboard without a number pad, connected via Bluetooth. It is backlit, is charged via USB-C and can switch between up to three devices at the press of a key.
