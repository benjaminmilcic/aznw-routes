# About Benjamin Milčić

## Who is Benjamin Milčić?
Benjamin Milčić is a German full stack developer living in Croatia. He develops web applications and mobile apps, mainly with Angular, Ionic and NestJS, and has also trained as an AI engineer. In his career he has mainly built enterprise applications. His website benjamin-milcic.dev presents his work, including a portfolio and many gimmicks.

## Where does Benjamin come from and where does he live?
Benjamin was born in 1978 in Halle an der Saale, Germany, as Benjamin Lüders. Since 2016 he has been living permanently in Croatia, on the coast. He is married to a Croatian woman and has a daughter. His wife works at the Croatian pension insurance fund. Benjamin's home address is private.

## Which languages does Benjamin speak?
German is Benjamin's native language. He also speaks excellent English (level C2) and advanced Croatian (level B2).

## How did Benjamin get into programming?
Benjamin has been programming since his youth. He started with Basic, then programmed in Turbo Pascal and Delphi. Later he built websites with HTML, CSS, JavaScript and PHP and started doing this commercially. During his computer science studies at Martin Luther University Halle-Wittenberg he earned money on the side by building websites. After his studies he took a break and worked in the tourism industry in Greece and Bulgaria. In 2019 he took online courses on Angular, Ionic and NestJS and returned to professional web and mobile app development. In 2026 he trained as an AI Engineer through an online course.

## Does Benjamin work with AI?
Yes. Driven by the AI hype, Benjamin trained as an AI engineer through online courses. He can build company-specific AI assistants and use large language models (LLMs) in projects, working with Python, the most common language in AI development. He also uses AI coding assistants such as Claude Code in his daily work. The AI chat on his website is one of his AI projects.

## What are Benjamin's hobbies?
Benjamin's hobbies are cycling and swimming. In his youth he played a lot of basketball and football.

## Which operating systems does Benjamin use?
Benjamin works with Windows and Linux.

## Where can you find Benjamin's code?
Benjamin has published a lot of private code as public repositories on GitHub (github.com/benjaminmilcic), including the source code of his website. The links are also in the footer of his website.
