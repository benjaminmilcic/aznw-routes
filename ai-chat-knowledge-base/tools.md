Tools of the AI chat, implemented in python-aznw-api (app/tools/):

- Availability (`get_availability`)
- Recipes (`search_recipes`)
- Jokes (`get_random_joke`)
- Guestbook (`find_guestbook_entries`, `get_guestbook_entry`)
- Weather (`get_weather`)
- Moorhuhn high scores (`get_moorhuhn_highscores`)
