# Portfolio (section of the home page)

## What is in Benjamin's portfolio?
The portfolio section shows selected projects as notes pinned to a pinboard. Clicking a project opens a dialog with a description, screenshots and links, for example to the live website, the app in the Google Play Store, a live demo, an APK download or the source code on GitHub. The projects include client projects Benjamin worked on for the software company Codext GmbH and his own projects. The details of each project are listed separately in the knowledge base.

## Which kinds of projects has Benjamin done?
Benjamin's projects range from mobile apps (iOS and Android with Ionic) to web dashboards with real-time data and multi-user apps with live sync. Many client projects are enterprise applications that can only be accessed with a login. That is why the portfolio also points to the gimmicks, small public apps and games that show what he can do.
