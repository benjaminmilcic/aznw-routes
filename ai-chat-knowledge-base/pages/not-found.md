# 404 page

## Does the website have a 404 page?
Yes. Every address that does not exist on the website shows a 404 page: a large "404", the message that the requested page was not found and two buttons – one to the home page and one to go back to the previous page. It has its own design with a picture. The page is also shown for unknown anchors on the home page (e.g. /#something) and for folders of the website such as /assets/. It can be opened directly at /404.
