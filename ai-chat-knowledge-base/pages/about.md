# About me (section of the home page)

## What is in the "About me" section of Benjamin's website?
The "About me" section describes Benjamin as a full stack developer for modern web and app development with a focus on performance – from the first idea to the finished app. Key figures: more than 10 years of experience, more than 20 projects and 100% passion. His core competencies are Angular, Ionic, NestJS and LLMs (large language models). The section also has a button to download his CV as a PDF in German, English or Croatian.

## How does Benjamin describe his way of working?
Benjamin says that over ten years of software development have taught him that code is only half the battle: it is about understanding problems, questioning requirements and delivering solutions that are still maintainable two years later. He works on frontends with Angular and Ionic and on backends with NestJS – from internal dashboards to production mobile apps with thousands of users. He values clean code and a clear component structure, tested with Cypress.
