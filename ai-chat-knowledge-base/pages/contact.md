# Contact (section of the home page)

## How can you contact Benjamin?
The easiest way is the contact form in the "Contact" section of the home page: enter your name, email and message and send it. Benjamin asks to describe the project as specifically as possible – stack, timeline, budget – and answers within 24 hours. Alternatively you can write to him by email at benjamin.milcic@gmail.com or call him at +49 176 41942532. Email and phone number can be copied with one click on the website.

## Is Benjamin available for new projects?
Whether Benjamin is currently available is shown by the badge at the top of the home page. For a new project, simply send him a message through the contact form.
