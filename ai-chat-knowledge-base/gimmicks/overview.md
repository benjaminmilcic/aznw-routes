# Overview (Gimmicks)

## What are the gimmicks on Benjamin's website?
The gimmicks are a collection of small apps Benjamin built in the evenings to try out new frameworks and APIs. In his job Benjamin has mainly developed enterprise applications that can only be accessed with a login and password. The gimmicks therefore show publicly what he can do. They include Weather, Map, Calendar, Guestbook, Charts, Countries, Jokes, Games, Recipes, Movies, AI Images and a Telegram web chat.

## How does the gimmick overview work?
On the gimmick overview every gimmick sits behind a door. Hovering over a door opens it, clicking it leads into the gimmick. Optionally each door shows a short description with the technologies and APIs used. For demonstration purposes there is a context menu above the doors (right-click) with settings: door sound on or off, show or hide the descriptions, and a background gradient with sliders for red, green, blue and opacity.

## In which languages are the gimmicks available?
All gimmicks are available in three languages: German, English and Croatian.
