# Moorhuhn (Game)

## What is Moorhuhn on Benjamin's website?
Moorhuhn is a remake of the well-known German shooting game (also known as Crazy Chicken): moorhens fly across the screen, and you try to shoot as many of them as possible with the mouse within 60 seconds.

## Is there a high score list in Moorhuhn?
Yes. After the game you can enter your name and appear in the high score list that all visitors can see. The list is stored by Benjamin's NestJS backend.
